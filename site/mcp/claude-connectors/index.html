Connect Gmail, Drive and Calendar to Claude with a sign-in

The easiest way in. Connect Google Drive, Gmail, Calendar and other apps to Claude by signing in. Nothing to install, no files, no code.

Heads-up · what this can touch

Safety: Acts in apps you connect: check each app's access

Only the apps you choose to connect, signed in as you. Each one gets whatever access its app asks for when you connect it, so read that list before you say yes. You can disconnect any of them in Claude's settings.

You'll need: A Claude account. Directory connectors work on every plan, Free included.

Where it falls short

  • Anthropic's help center states no default for whether Claude asks before acting, so check each connector's tool settings yourself and set anything that sends, creates or deletes to Needs approval.
  • On Team and Enterprise plans an Owner has to enable each connector before anyone on the team can use it.
  • Free accounts can add only one custom connector (one that isn't in Claude's directory), and installing connectors from the mobile app is still in beta.

What it is

Connectors are MCP servers that the app makers host for you. You don’t install anything: you click connect, sign in to the app, and Claude can search and read it when you ask.

Set it up

  1. In Claude, open Customize → Connectors, then click + to browse the directory.
  2. Pick an app, for example Google Drive, and click Connect.
  3. Sign in to that app and approve the access it asks for. Read the list before you approve.
  4. Open the connector’s settings and look at its tools. Each one can be set to Always allow, Needs approval or Blocked. Set anything that sends, creates or deletes to Needs approval.
  5. In a new chat, ask something that needs it: “Find the proposal I sent last month and summarize the pricing section.”

On a Team or Enterprise plan, an admin turns connectors on first under Organization settings → Connectors.

Try these

  • “What’s on my calendar Thursday, and what do I need to prep for each meeting?”
  • “Search my Drive for anything about the lease renewal and give me the key dates.”
  • “Draft a reply to the last email from [NAME], but don’t send it.”

Good to know

  • Directory connectors are available on every plan, Free included. Free accounts can also add one custom connector.
  • If your business uses a work Google or Microsoft account, your admin may need to allow it.
  • We haven’t run these setup steps ourselves yet; we checked them against Anthropic’s help center on Nov 1, 2026. Menu names change as Claude updates. If you can’t find it, search the Claude help center for “connectors”.
