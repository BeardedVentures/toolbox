Build a one-page site that turns QR scans into leads

# Lead-capture page

Build ONE static HTML file (inline CSS and JS, no framework) that loads fast on a phone and does one job: collect a lead.

## Before building, get from the person
- The offer, in the visitor's words ("What's my home worth?")
- Their name, title, phone, email, brand color and logo or headshot (they'll swap in the real files)
- Where leads should go: an email address (via a form backend like Formspree), or an automation webhook (Zapier, Make, n8n). If they only use Google Forms or Tally, link or embed that form instead of building a custom one.

## Page structure, top to bottom
1. Headline that asks the visitor's question, with one word in the brand color.
2. One line saying what happens after they submit and how soon.
3. A form with 4 to 6 fields only. Example for home value: address, name, phone, email, and "When are you thinking of selling?" (ASAP / 1-3 months / 3-6 months / 6-12 months / just curious).
4. One full-width button that names the outcome ("Get my home value"), with a "Sending..." state.
5. A consent line under the button ("By submitting, you agree to be contacted about your request.") and a link to a short privacy note.
6. Footer with the person's name, title, tap-to-call and tap-to-email links, and logo.

## Build rules
- Inputs at 16px or larger so phones don't zoom. Real `autocomplete` values (name, tel, email, street-address). Visible labels, not placeholder-only.
- A hidden honeypot field; drop any submission that fills it.
- Send the form as JSON (with `Accept: application/json`) to the form backend or webhook, and treat any non-2xx response as a failure. Add `source` (for example "qr-flyer") and a timestamp to every submission.
- If sending fails, show a message and open a pre-filled email to the person in the visitor's mail app, so the lead isn't lost.
- Show success and error messages on the page, without reloading.
- Put the person's brand color in `<meta name="theme-color">`.
- No tracking scripts unless the person asks, and then say what each one collects.

## After building
- Tell the person how to host it free (GitHub Pages, Netlify or Cloudflare Pages) and to give it a permanent address BEFORE printing the QR code.
- Give them a 5-point test list: submit from a phone, submit with the honeypot filled, turn on airplane mode and submit, check the lead arrives, scan the printed QR code.
Save it in a folder named lead-capture-page.

Where it falls short

  • The honeypot check runs only in the page's own script, so a bot posting straight to the Formspree endpoint skips it; the skill doesn't mention Formspree's own spam field.
  • The mailto backup still does nothing visible on a phone with no mail app set up, so a failed send can still lose the lead.
  • The page promises 'Sam will text or call you', but the consent line the skill dictates only covers being 'contacted', and the skill leaves the content of the privacy note it links to undefined.

When to use it

You need a page for a yard sign, flyer, business card or truck wrap QR code, and a full website is overkill. Works for “what’s my home worth?”, “free estimate”, “join the waitlist” or “sign up for the association”.

Where this came from

We built a page like this for a real-estate agent’s home-valuation leads, plus a membership sign-up on the same pattern. The structure and the “never lose a lead” backup came from that build. The consent line is an improvement we’d add next time.

Install it

  • Claude Code: save as ~/.claude/skills/lead-capture-page/SKILL.md, then ask “build me a lead page for my home valuation QR code”.
  • Claude, ChatGPT: paste the part below the second --- into a Project or Custom GPT, or straight into a chat, then answer its questions.

Before you go live

Check your local rules for collecting contact details and texting leads, and add your brokerage’s or business’s required disclosures.
