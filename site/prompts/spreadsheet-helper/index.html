Prompt 01-19

Get the spreadsheet formula you need, explained

Describe what you want your spreadsheet to do in normal words. Get the formula, where to put it, and how it works.

BeginnerTestedWorks in any AI chatSmall businessSolo operatorFamily & home
Prompt
I'm using [EXCEL OR GOOGLE SHEETS] (if Excel, say 365 or the year, since some functions need it).

My data looks like this: [DESCRIBE YOUR COLUMNS]
(for example: "A is date, B is customer name, C is amount, D is paid yes/no")

What I want: [WHAT YOU WANT]
(for example: "total unpaid amount per customer for this month")

Give me:
1. The exact formula, ready to paste, and which cell to put it in
2. A one-line explanation of what each part does
3. The most likely reason it would show an error or quietly give a wrong total (like dates or numbers stored as text), and how to check for it
4. If a pivot table or filter would be easier than a formula, say so and give the steps instead
Fill in
  • [EXCEL OR GOOGLE SHEETS]
  • [DESCRIBE YOUR COLUMNS]
  • [WHAT YOU WANT]

Paste your own words, not client records, passwords or anything confidential. What you paste goes to the AI company.

What it actually does

Tested in Claude · Nov 1, 2026

Run by Claude while building this page. The example below is from that run, trimmed.

We filled in
EXCEL OR GOOGLE SHEETS: Google Sheets DESCRIBE YOUR COLUMNS: A is job date, B is customer name, C is amount invoiced, D is paid (Yes/No). Data starts in row 2. WHAT YOU WANT: total unpaid amount per customer for this month
It came back with (trimmed)
1. The formula In F2, list each customer once: `` =UNIQUE(FILTER(B2:B, B2:B<>"")) ` In G2, then drag down beside the list: ` =SUMIFS(C:C, B:B, F2, D:D, "No", A:A, ">="&(EOMONTH(TODAY(),-1)+1), A:A, "<"&(EOMONTH(TODAY(),0)+1)) ` 2. What each part does - SUMIFS(C:C, …) adds the amounts in C where every condition matches. - D:D, "No": only unpaid rows. - EOMONTH(TODAY(),-1)+1: the 1st of this month… 3. Most likely problem: a quietly wrong total If any dates were pasted in as text, SUMIFS skips those rows and the total comes out low with no error. Same for "No " with a trailing space. Check: in a spare cell, =ISNUMBER(A2); FALSE means that date is text. =SUMPRODUCT(--(TRIM(D2:D)<>D2:D)) counts paid cells with extra spaces. (A #REF!` in F2 means something is blocking the list below it.) 4. Easier option Insert → Pivot table → Rows: Customer → Values: Amount (SUM) → Filters: Paid = No, and Date → Filter by condition → "Date is after" the last day of last month…

Where it falls short

  • The text-date check (=ISNUMBER(A2)) tests one cell; the user has to fill it down the column to find every bad row.
  • TODAY() makes the total follow the calendar, so on the 1st last month's unpaid total disappears; there is no cell to pick a month.
  • The pivot's date filter is a fixed date the user must change every month, unlike the formula.

When to use it

Any time you think “there has to be a formula for this.”

Make it better

Paste the first 5 rows of your sheet (with names changed) instead of describing the columns. The answer will be exact.
